# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack
Existing: static HTML generated by `build-directions.mjs` from `carte.tpl.html` (canvas 2D + WebGL2 relief), hosted on GitHub Pages at unknownpedia.org. No framework, no dependencies.

## Users
Primary: the curious person with no specialist background (lycéen, étudiant, grand public), French or English speaking. Experts are not the audience but the reviewers: they will only share the site if they find no error in it.

## Product Purpose
Unknownpedia is an open atlas of what humanity does not know yet. Open scientific questions are placed on a measured map of world research (4,516 OpenAlex topics, positioned by shared articles). You enter out of curiosity, wander, and leave knowing where one could help. Success: a visitor understands in seconds that the dark edges of the map are real, sourced unknowns, and wants to read a question and share it.

## Positioning
A cross-science, public-facing, bilingual, open-data registry of open questions whose position on the map is measured, not drawn by hand, with every claim sourced and the AI's role displayed on each page. Neighbours: the Scientific Challenge Atlas (University of Michigan, proposed), the Erdős problems database (mathematics only).

## Operating Context
Visitors arrive from shared links, one question at a time (per-question pages) or via the map. Content is bilingual FR/EN. Data comes from OpenAlex (CC0) and Wikipedia extracts (CC BY-SA); everything must be citable and openly licensed. One author, helped by an AI; drafts are AI-written and labeled as such until a named specialist has reviewed them.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Map with measured relief (research effort, not knowledge), 4,516 topics, 105 questions (target 100–150), 12 full fiches with verified references.
- Provenance label on every fiche ("draft written with an AI, references checked, not yet reviewed" or "reviewed by NAME, date").
- All fonts, images and code must be under free licences (OFL, CC0…), credited on a sources-and-licences page reachable from a small bottom-right info bubble.
- No accounts, votes or rankings at launch. No advertising, ever.
- Undecided: light vs dark vs both; analytics tool; repository licence.

## Brand Commitments
Name: Unknownpedia (unknownpedia.org, .com redirects). The "?" marking a question on the map stays. The top-left context text (what you are looking at) stays and fades out on zoom; its wording and typeface may change. Honesty over polish: never present research effort as knowledge, never hide AI use.

## Evidence on Hand
Real data in `squelette.json`, `liens-sujets.json`, `questions.json`, `fiches.json`. A working map and 3 per-question pages. No testimonials, press or traffic data: do not invent any.

## Product Principles
1. Every visual element encodes a real datum or a real source; nothing decorative.
2. The unknown is shown at the edge of the known, never as empty space for its own sake.
3. Say what the AI did and what a human checked, on the page, always.
4. Sober and precise first; attractive second, never cheap or game-like.
5. Open by default: open data, open licences, openly credited.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
French and English parity. Keyboard access is currently missing (canvas); reading pages must work without JavaScript. Scientists often prefer light backgrounds: contrast and legibility must hold in whichever themes ship.
