Nobel Prize in Medicine 2023: mRNA vaccines

Major advance · accepted · 2023

Katalin Karikó and Drew Weissman were honoured for nucleoside base modifications that enabled effective mRNA vaccines against COVID-19. Their 2005 paper showed that replacing uridine with pseudouridine made mRNA non-immunogenic.
