IceCube: neutrinos from astrophysical sources

Major advance · accepted · 2026

IceCube reported high-energy neutrinos from outside the Galaxy in 2013, linked one to the blazar TXS 0506+056 (2018), and found emission from NGC 1068 (2022) and the Milky Way plane (2023). Wikipedia lists Francis Halzen as the 2026 Nobel laureate in Physics for it; we could not check this against the Nobel Committee.
