What is the solution to the last passage of Kryptos?

Documented enigma

An encrypted sculpture of 1990 outside CIA headquarters. Three of four passages have been deciphered; the last is considered unsolved.

Draft written with an AI (Claude). The references exist, checked in OpenAlex; the text has not yet been reviewed by a specialist. Who writes what

What we know

  • Kryptos is a sculpture by Jim Sanborn, installed in 1990 outside CIA headquarters in Langley. It carries four encrypted messages.
  • Three of the four passages have been deciphered, and the solutions made public.
  • The last passage, called K4, is considered unsolved.

What we don't know

  • The solution to K4 and the encryption method used for it.
  • Whether the clues published by the artist are enough to get there.

Why it's hard

  • The passage is short, so there is little material for statistical analysis.
  • We do not know which method was used, which forces trying them all.

Why it matters

It is a living example of cryptanalysis: a public puzzle with clear rules, used as an exercise for more than thirty years.
