AI solves some obscure Erdős problems

Major advance · accepted · 2026 · AI

In a Gemini case study (arXiv, January 2026), 24 authors screened 700 open Erdős problems. Of 13 addressed, 5 gave apparently new autonomous solutions and 8 matched existing literature. The authors say the problems were open mainly because they were obscure. An earlier claim that GPT-5 solved ten problems was called a misrepresentation by Thomas Bloom: it had found existing solutions.
