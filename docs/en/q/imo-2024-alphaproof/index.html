AlphaProof and AlphaGeometry 2 at the 2024 Olympiad

Major advance · accepted · 2024 · AI

DeepMind says its two systems solved 4 of 6 problems of the 2024 International Mathematical Olympiad, 28 of 42 points, one point under the gold threshold. Humans first translated the problems into formal language, and some took up to three days. Source: DeepMind's own announcement.
