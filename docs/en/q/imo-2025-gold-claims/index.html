Olympiad 2025: two gold-level claims

Major advance · disputed · 2025 · AI

DeepMind's Gemini Deep Think scored 35 of 42 in natural language, graded by the IMO coordinators, who say this does not validate the system itself. OpenAI claimed the same score for an experimental model graded by the company, not the IMO. Terence Tao and Kevin Buzzard said such claims are not reproducible or comparable with students.
