AlphaEvolve: records in sphere packing and matrix multiplication

Progress with AI help · 2025-05-14

Google DeepMind says AlphaEvolve, a Gemini-powered coding agent, found an algorithm multiplying two 4×4 complex matrices with 48 scalar multiplications (better than Strassen, 1969, in that setting) and a configuration of 593 spheres setting a new lower bound for the kissing number in 11 dimensions. On more than 50 open problems it reportedly matched the state of the art about 75% of the time and improved it in 20%. These are improved bounds, not answers to the questions themselves. Source: the lab's own announcement (not peer-reviewed here; we did not check the associated paper).
