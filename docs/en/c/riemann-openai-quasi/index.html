Riemann: the “quasi-Riemann hypothesis” (OpenAI)

Announced, not accepted · 2026-10-07

In early October 2026 OpenAI released 722 manuscripts from an unreleased internal model, including “The Quasi-Riemann Hypothesis”, about a zero-free region for L-functions. According to Quanta, one version puts the zeros between 1/8 and 7/8 (199 pages, Lean-verified) and another between 1/12 and 11/12 (49 pages, human-edited, not yet Lean-verified); one outlet notes a mismatch between these figures that we could not resolve. Experts largely think the result is correct, but Peter Sarnak will not call it a theorem until humans check the details, and Will Sawin and Thomas Bloom doubt it reaches the 1/2 line. This is not the Riemann hypothesis.
