Erdős unit distance conjecture: a counterexample announced

Announced, not accepted · 2026-05-20

On 20 May 2026 OpenAI announced that an internal, non-public model had found a counterexample to Erdős's 1946 unit distance conjecture. According to Quanta, the result was not definitive: mathematicians substantially improved it within weeks, and a paper by nine mathematicians comments on the proof's correctness. Quanta quotes Timothy Gowers: had a human submitted it to the Annals of Mathematics, he would have recommended acceptance without hesitation. We found no formal verification; we only read Quanta's account.
