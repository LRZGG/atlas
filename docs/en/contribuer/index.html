How to contribute

No accounts on the site, no votes, no rankings: you contribute through GitHub, and a correction is judged on its sources.

What you can do

  • Correct a fiche or challenge a claim: a wrong reference, a number without a source, an overstated sentence. Attach a link or a DOI.
  • Propose a question: it needs a source stating it is unresolved.
  • Review a fiche if you are a specialist in the field: your name and the date are shown on the reviewed fiche.

How fiches are checked

  • Each fiche is a draft written with an AI. References are checked in OpenAlex by a program, and Wikipedia excerpts are credited.
  • An automatic check (no AI) flags what a human should look at first: numbers missing from the sources, strong claims, references possibly off-topic, gaps between French and English.
  • A flagged point is not an error: it is a question put to a human. Decisions (who ruled, when) are logged in controle/decisions.json.
  • No fiche is marked “reviewed” without a named person.

Rules

  • An extraordinary claim needs a solid source. A popular hypothesis is not a credible hypothesis.
  • No personal data, no accusations, no medical advice.
  • The relief of the map measures research effort (papers published), not knowledge.
